Our study explores the application of Temporal Fusion Transformers (TFT) for multivariate, multi-step traffic prediction. By integrating quantile probabilistic forecasting, we demonstrate a significant improvement in predictive accuracy, with a precision of approximately 95%, outperforming established deep learning models such as LSTMs, Vanilla Transformers, and Informers.[See More](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13177-025-00480-1)
